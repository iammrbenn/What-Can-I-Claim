Privacy

Pilot version. Replace [OPERATOR] with your company name and ICO registration number before launch.

Your answers stay on your device

Everything you enter, including income, savings, health and caring details, is stored only in your browser on this device. It is never sent to our servers. The eligibility check runs on your device.

Postcode lookup

To find your council, your postcode is sent to postcodes.io, a free public postcode service. Nothing else is sent with it.

Anonymous usage statistics

We record anonymous events so we and the organisation that gave you this link can see whether the service helps people. These are: that a check was started or finished, which schemes were suggested, the rough total value found, which "apply" links were clicked, and statuses you set in your wallet.

Each device gets a random ID so repeat visits are not counted twice. We do not record your name, contact details, postcode, income, savings, health, IP address or exact time. Organisations only see totals, and totals under 5 are hidden.

Payments

If you pay to unlock the app, payment is handled by Stripe, who receive your email and card details under their own privacy policy. We store only your unlock code, the Stripe payment reference, and the random IDs of devices where the code has been used (up to 5). Your answers are never linked to your payment.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete my answers" on the results or wallet page, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Your unlock code stays on the device so you do not lose access.
